1. About you

Before you scan

  • Clothes: one T-shirt (fitted is better), no hoodie or jacket. Barefoot. Hair off your shoulders. Empty pockets.
  • Device: upright on a shelf or table at about waist height, screen not tilted back. Stand 2 to 3 m away. Good light, plain background, nobody else in view.
  • Front pose: face the camera, arms straight about 30 to 45° out from your body, feet hip-width apart, breathe normally.
  • Side pose: turn to your left so your side faces the camera, arms straight down, hands by your thighs, feet together.
  • The scan captures by itself once the pose is right, and tells you what to fix.
